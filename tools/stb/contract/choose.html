These numbers belong to this machine. The rasters are drawn by this browser, on this OS, at 1× pixel density. macOS ignores font hinting and places stems at fractional pixels; Windows and Linux hint and snap to the grid — so cells near the threshold will land differently on other platforms, and a high-density display separates every pair better than the 1× worst case measured here.

What is universal: a 100% identical verdict from shared outlines, shape facts like a serifed 1 or a slashed zero, and the whole weight-truth band below — face substitution for a missing weight is CSS-specified behaviour on every platform.
